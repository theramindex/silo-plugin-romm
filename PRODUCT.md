# RomM for Silo

A full-page Games client within the existing Ramindex Silo family. People use
keyboard, touch, TV remotes and gamepads to browse their RomM collection, play
supported classic systems, and resume personal progress across browser sessions.

The visual system follows Silo's theme and readability preferences, using the
family's bundled Plus Jakarta Sans. Games and their cover art lead the library;
compact controls and a dedicated player keep navigation predictable. Forms
explain account linking directly. Loading, unsupported platforms, unavailable
saves and pending synchronization stay visible and actionable.

Each verified Silo account/profile links explicitly to its own RomM identity.
Credentials stay on the server, links persist encrypted, and player tickets,
local storage and upload ownership remain bound to that connection. No iframe,
name-based user matching, shared admin credentials or browser token exposure.

The Games header and navigation stay visible while browsing. Scoped search and a
compact toolbar prioritize covers; mobile secondary filters expand on demand.
Pinned/recent platforms are local connection preferences. Continue playing merges
RomM’s per-user history with successful Silo starts saved locally for the linked
connection and opens the exact played ROM. Resume appears only
when compatible progress is known; checking details never reserves a game session.
